Governance & ML Principles

Why SmartBank AI?

Built strictly according to enterprise banking ML governance standards.

01

Leakage Safe

Excludes duration, contact, day, month, and campaign. Predictions use only parameters available strictly before initiating outreach.

02

Imbalance Aware

With only 11.7% historical subscribers, models use class_weight='balanced' and are evaluated on Precision, Recall, F1, and ROC-AUC rather than misleading naive accuracy.

03

Actionable Tiers

Converts ML probabilities into 0–100 Opportunity Scores with actionable triage (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW) to maximize bank relationship manager conversion hours.

04

Explainable Signals

Extracts clear feature associations and model coefficients while explicitly distinguishing predictive correlation from causal claims.
