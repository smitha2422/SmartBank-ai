🛡️ Leakage Prevention Architecture

Why are certain dataset fields strictly excluded?

SmartBank AI is designed to operate immediately before initiating the current campaign contact. Features that only become observable during or as a result of that interaction are prohibited to prevent target leakage.

Excluded Feature Reason for Exclusion
duration Call duration in seconds. Unknown prior to calling. A 15-minute call strongly correlates with subscription, but cannot be known beforehand.
contact Communication channel of current interaction (cellular / telephone).
day, month Specific timing of the current call outreach.
campaign Number of contacts performed during the current campaign.
