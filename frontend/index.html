Selection Rationale:

Random Forest was selected as the final production pipeline because it achieved higher F1-score (0.3835) and ROC-AUC (0.7445) on minority positive subscribers while reducing False Positives from 2,086 down to 1,484.

Final Test Confusion Matrix (20% Isolated Partition)

False Positive (FP) 1,484 Call made, did not subscribe

Business Cost Analysis:
  • False Positive (FP): Cost of a 5-minute agent call (~€2–€5). Acceptable tradeoff to capture high-value deposit assets.
  • False Negative (FN): Cost of missed term deposit margin (€200–€500 lifetime value). Minimized by balanced class weighting.

🛡️ Pre-Contact Leakage Guard Architecture

In bank telemarketing, calculating conversion propensity during or after a call provides zero operational value for campaign planning.

Excluded Leakage Features (5 Total):

  • duration: Call duration is only known after the phone call concludes. (High correlation with subscription = massive data leakage).
  • contact: Communication channel assigned for current outreach.
  • day, month: Timing/scheduling of current outreach.
  • campaign: Number of contacts performed during the current campaign.

Active Pre-Contact Features (11 Total):

Demographics (age, job, marital, education), Financial Health (balance, default, housing, loan), and Historical Prior Campaign Interactions (poutcome, pdays, previous).
