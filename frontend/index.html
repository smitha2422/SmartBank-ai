🛡️ Leakage Prevention Architecture

Why are certain dataset fields strictly excluded?

SmartBank AI is designed to operate immediately before initiating the current campaign contact. Features that only become observable during or as a result of that interaction are prohibited to prevent target leakage.

Excluded FeatureReason for Exclusion
durationCall duration in seconds. Unknown prior to calling. A 15-minute call strongly correlates with subscription, but cannot be known beforehand.
contactCommunication channel of current interaction (cellular / telephone).
day, monthSpecific timing of the current call outreach.
campaignNumber of contacts performed during current campaign cycle.
